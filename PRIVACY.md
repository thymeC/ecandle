## English

eCandle (“the App”) is developed by JING CHEN.

### Summary

eCandle does **not** collect, store, or share your personal information on our servers. Everything stays on your device.

### Information we collect

We do **not** collect personal data. The App works offline and does not require an account.

### Microphone

The App may request microphone access to detect blow strength (volume) so you can blow out the candles. Audio is processed **locally on device only**. We do **not** record, store, or upload microphone audio.

### Data stored on your device

The App may store settings locally on your iPhone or iPad (via iOS app storage), such as candle count preferences and music on/off. This data is not transmitted to us and is removed if you delete the App.

### Third-party services

The App does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

### Children

The App is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from children.

### Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.

## 简体中文

电子蜡烛（“本应用”）由 JING CHEN 开发。

### 概要

电子蜡烛**不会**在我们的服务器上收集、存储或分享您的个人信息。数据仅保存在您的设备上。

### 我们收集的信息

我们**不**收集个人数据。本应用可离线使用，无需注册账号。

### 麦克风

本应用可能请求麦克风权限，用于检测吹气音量以熄灭蜡烛。音频仅在**本机本地处理**。我们**不会**录音、保存或上传麦克风音频。

### 设备本地存储

本应用可能在您的 iPhone 或 iPad 上本地保存设置（如蜡烛数量偏好、音乐开关）。这些数据不会上传给我们；删除应用后会被清除。

### 第三方服务

本应用不使用第三方分析、广告或追踪 SDK。

### 儿童

本应用面向一般用户，不会故意收集儿童信息。

### 政策变更

我们可能更新本政策；更新时会修改上方“Last updated”日期。
