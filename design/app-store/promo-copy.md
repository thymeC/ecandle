### 描述

```
电子蜡烛 —— 没有打火机，也能郑重许愿。

点亮蛋糕上的蜡烛，对着 iPhone 底部麦克风轻轻吹气，火焰会一根根熄灭。全部吹灭后，彩纸飞舞，「生日快乐」响起。

■ 多根蜡烛或数字蜡烛
按数量摆放，或按年龄显示成数字造型蜡烛（例如 25 → 「2」和「5」）。

■ 麦克风吹气
点燃后自动进入沉浸模式并开启吹气检测。安静环境下对着话筒吹，强度够了就会熄灭。

■ 生日快乐背景音乐
可开关的合成版经典《生日快乐》旋律，点燃时响起，吹灭后停止。

■ 再吹一次
庆祝结束后可一键重新布置、再点亮，适合连着给好几个人过生日。

麦克风仅用于检测吹气音量，音频不会上传。无需账号，完全离线可用。
```

## English (en-US)

### Name

```
eCandle
```

### Subtitle

```
Blow out birthday candles with your mic
```

### Promotional Text

```
No lighter needed: light the cake, blow into your iPhone, and celebrate with confetti and Happy Birthday.
```

### Keywords

```
birthday,candle,blow out,cake,party,wish,microphone,celebrate,fun,kids
```

### Description

```
eCandle — make a wish without a lighter.

Light the candles on a cake, then blow into your iPhone microphone to put them out one by one. When the last flame goes out, confetti falls and Happy Birthday plays.

• Count candles or digit-shaped candles by age
• Mic blow detection after lighting
• Optional Happy Birthday background music
• Replay anytime for the next celebration

Microphone audio is used only for local volume detection and is never uploaded. No account required. Works offline.
```
